# Saying no to everything else

> [返回中文译版](./index.md) · Source: [https://sive.rs/no2](https://sive.rs/no2) · Author: Derek Sivers (September 23, 2015)

![Saying no to everything else](/images/no2/cover.webp)

[Steven Pressfield](https://stevenpressfield.com/about/) called himself an author for years, but he’d never actually finished a book. Eventually, the psychological pain of not finishing kept building until he couldn’t stand it anymore. He decided to finally beat the devil he calls “The Resistance”.

He created a situation with no escape. He rented a cabin, brought his typewriter, and shut off all other options.

He said,

> “I didn’t talk to anybody during that year… I didn’t hang out. I just worked. I had a book in mind and I had decided I would finish it or kill myself. I could not run away again, or let people down again, or let myself down again. This was it, do or die.”

After a difficult year of wrestling with those inner demons and avoiding all temptations, he did it. He finished his first book. It wasn’t a success, but it didn’t matter. He had finally beat The Resistance. He went on to write many successful novels.

He told this story in the great book *[Turning Pro](https://sive.rs/book/TurningPro)*, the third in his series of little books about the creative struggle, including *[The War of Art](https://sive.rs/book/WarOfArt)* and *[Do the Work](https://sive.rs/book/DoTheWork)*. Read all three.

“[Hell yeah or no](/translations/hellyeah/)” is a filter you can use to decide what’s worth doing. But this is simpler and more serious. **This is a decision to stop deciding.** It’s one decision, in advance, that the answer to all future distractions is “no” until you finish what you started. **It’s saying yes to one thing, and no to absolutely everything else.**
